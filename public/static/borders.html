Shape is a design choice.
Adjacency is a rule.

The live game checks an explicit army/fleet connection table, including separate coasts. It never guesses movement from whether two pixels touch. The audit found no differences from webDiplomacy’s Classic data across 222 directed army connections and 282 directed fleet connections.

These are visual concepts. The angular versions have not replaced the live board. A final playable angular map would need shared-edge and small-province checks, especially around Denmark, Constantinople, and split coasts.

My recommendation: keep organic coastlines and use restrained, straighter interior borders. It gives the board a cleaner strategy-map feel while retaining its atlas character.
