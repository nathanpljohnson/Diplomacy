HOST ADJUDICATION

Resolve this phase?

Orders are revealed and resolved simultaneously. This cannot be undone. Unordered units hold; missing retreats disband; missing builds are waived. Required removals use the engine’s civil disorder rules. For a drunk country, a submitted movement proposal is selected randomly; retreats, builds, and required removals are automatic.
